# Camera Lost & Found

**Category:** Misc · **Points:** 100 · **Challenge ID:** 19

---

**Brief.** An abandoned camera's photos from an India trip; something is hidden
in one of them.

**Path.**

1. Extract `2026-india-trip.7z` (50 JPEGs).
2. Most images have ordinary `ImageDescription` values. One file,
   `IMG_38.jpg`, has a URL instead:

   ```bash
   exiftool -ImageDescription IMG_38.jpg
   # https://ctf.pir4cy.com/exif-flag
   ```

3. Fetch the URL. The page returns:

   ```text
   SW5JdHtoMWQxbmdfMW5fbTN0YWQ0dDR9Cg==
   ```

   which base64-decodes to the flag.

**Flag.** `InIt{h1d1ng_1n_m3tad4t4}`

**Takeaway.** Bulk `exiftool` over the whole folder; the odd one out was a URL in
the EXIF description. The page again had a hidden injection decoy.
