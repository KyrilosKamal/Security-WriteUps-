# Where Am I?

**Category:** OSINT · **Points:** 100 · **Challenge ID:** 2

**Brief:** Kidnap photo. Find city, country, timezone abbrev during DST.

**Steps:**

1. `file` say WEBP, not JPG. Convert: `Image.open(...).convert("RGB").save("where.png")`.
2. Stone arch, stepped street, medieval brick. Landmark = Acquedotto Medievale, Perugia, Umbria, Italy.
3. TZ `Europe/Rome`. DST abbrev = CEST (UTC+2). Not CET.
4. Format: `InIt{CITY_COUNTRY_TIMEZONE}`, caps, underscores.

**Flag:** `InIt{PERUGIA_ITALY_CEST}`

**Takeaway:** Landmark ID + DST. CEST, not CET.
